## Synthesis

**Safe candidates that add actual value:** None of the eight is ready for direct production adoption based on these inspections. **Animated Beam** is the sole plausible optional enhancement: it has verified MIT terms and could explain integrations or relationships in a small, noncritical panel, but only after resolving the separate `motion/react` dependency compatibility and adding reduced-motion/accessibility safeguards. The existing Radix-based primitives and current dashboard patterns remain the safer choices for dialogs, tabs, and operational actions.

**Exclude from the operational casework UI:** Aceternity components are excluded pending exact component-level license/notice verification; the Animated Sidebar is additionally unavailable at its requested official URL. Card Spotlight, Background Beams, and Progressive Blur are mainly decorative and risk distracting from or obscuring dense case data. Stateful Button’s async feedback is potentially useful but cannot be safely adopted without verified source/license and accessible loading/success/error behavior. No license uncertainty should be resolved by assuming permissive terms from a vendor’s branding or copy-paste installation instructions.
