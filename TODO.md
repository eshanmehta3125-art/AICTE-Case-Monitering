## 2. Overview and operational dashboard — Complete

- Give the “Coordination Required” section a prominent but restrained presentation. The example ABC Institute of Technology / `DEMO-10024` may show PGRC grievance under process and RB regulatory review initiated, matched by same institution ID, coordination status pending review. Do not automatically label legitimate parallel actions as duplicate complaints; potential conflicts require human review.

Charts must answer operational questions, provide useful labels/tooltips, and agree with the underlying dataset. Do not fill the page with unnecessary decoration or oversized empty charts/KPIs.

## 5. Explainable overlap detection and human review — Complete

- Build a dedicated Overlap Review Centre showing potential candidates across Bureaus/Cells. For each candidate include institution name and Permanent ID, all associated case IDs, involved Bureaus/Cells, current statuses and assigned officers, complaint categories, dates received and last-action dates, reason records were matched, match confidence or review priority where available, suggested coordination action, and review status.
- Provide side-by-side case comparison so an official can compare records without repeatedly opening separate screens. For the example ABC Institute of Technology, compare PGRC case `PGRC-2026-0142` and RB case `RB-2026-0081`, including category, status, and institution ID. Display the explanation: “Both records reference the same institution. Separate actions may be in progress. Human review is required to determine whether coordination is necessary.”
- Matching must be explainable and deterministic. Apply this priority: exact AICTE Permanent ID; exact institution identifier or mapped institution record; normalized institution-name match; fuzzy institution-name match only to identify likely spelling/format variations; similar complaint descriptions or case references only as secondary supporting evidence. Institution identity is the main signal. Similar descriptions alone must not establish that two complaints concern the same institution. If semantic/AI integration is not configured, do not pretend it exists.
- Use human-readable match reasons such as “Matched using the same AICTE Permanent ID.”, “Possible institution-name variation. Manual verification required.”, and “Same institution with multiple active Bureaus/Cells.”
- Keep these concepts distinct: related cases (records concern the same institution or issue); potential overlap (parallel actions may need coordination); confirmed duplicate (a reviewer verified the same underlying complaint); and reviewed — no conflict (multiple actions are legitimate and need no further coordination). Multiple complaints from one institution are not automatically duplicates.
- Provide actions to confirm related cases, mark reviewed — no conflict, link cases, record coordination note, assign coordinating officer, and escalate for resolution. Record outcomes, reviewer, and action history. Do not automatically merge separate complaints or change their official statuses when a potential overlap is detected.
